Privacy

What happens when you visit bimuti.com.

What this website collects

Nothing. There are no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no adverts and no accounts. The page loads only its own files: no outside scripts or fonts.

Our hosts

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which secures the connection and protects the site. Like most web services, GitHub and Cloudflare may log visitors' IP addresses for security, and Cloudflare may receive reports if a page fails to load; see their own privacy statements. We do not receive or look at visitor logs.

Share button and PeMA beta code

The Share button uses your device's own share menu, or copies the link. The PeMA beta code is checked on your device; it is not sent or stored anywhere.

Our apps

Each app lives on its own address, for example rentuk.bimuti.com, so each app's data is kept separate on your device.
